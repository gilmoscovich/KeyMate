# KeyMate

[עברית](README.md)

Moved from Windows to Mac and miss your familiar keyboard shortcuts? KeyMate helps you quickly find the matching Mac shortcut in Hebrew or English.

## Download and install

1. Download the [latest release](https://github.com/gilmoscovich/KeyMate/releases/latest).
2. Open the DMG file.
3. Drag `KeyMate` to the `Applications` folder.
4. Open KeyMate and click the keyboard icon in the menu bar.

## How to use it

Describe the action you want to perform in natural language and KeyMate shows you the matching Mac shortcut.

Examples:

- `take a screenshot`
- `delete a word`
- `reopen closed tab`
- `how do I force quit`
- `ctrl shift t`

You can also select a category, switch between Light, Dark, and System appearance, and revisit recent searches.

## Privacy

Your searches and the shortcut catalog stay on your Mac. KeyMate does not send search queries over the network and does not require an account.

## Requirements

macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
